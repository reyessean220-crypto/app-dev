# Frozen (2013)
**Frozen** is pure Disney magic. It is a story about sisterhood, self-acceptance, and the power of love.
> "Let it go, let it go  
> Can't hold it back anymore!"  
> — Elsa
## Why It’s Special
- Incredible original songs
- Beautiful animation
- Deep emotional story about family
- Elsa is one of the most iconic Disney characters
---
## Main Characters
1. **Elsa** - Snow Queen with ice powers (voiced by Idina Menzel)
2. **Anna** - Fearless younger sister (voiced by Kristen Bell)
3. **Kristoff** - Ice harvester (voiced by Jonathan Groff)
4. **Olaf** - Living snowman (voiced by Josh Gad)
## Iconic Songs
- **Let It Go**
- *Do You Want to Build a Snowman?*
- For the First Time in Forever
- Love Is an Open Door
## Quick Facts
- Won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature
- Became one of the highest-grossing animated films
- Inspired sequels and a Broadway musical
- Still makes people cry during the emotional scenes
That moment when Anna sacrifices herself and true love turns out to be sisterly love is unforgettable.
